How It Works

Smart Campus Dining in 3 Easy Steps

1. Select Food & Slot

Browse the digital canteen menu, choose your favorite dishes and pick your preferred pickup time slot.

2. Live Kitchen Prep

Canteen staff receives your order instantly on their dashboard and begins preparing your hot meal.

3. Fast Express Pickup

Get notified when your meal is ready. Flash your unique Order ID at the counter and enjoy your food!
