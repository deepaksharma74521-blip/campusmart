Real-Time Visibility & Audio Engine

Live 4-Stage Status Tracking & Voice Alerts

Harmonic Web Audio chimes + Text-To-Speech synthesized announcements

1. Placed

Order registered in MongoDB database.

2. Preparing

Kitchen cooks or mart staff packs items.

3. Ready for Pickup

4-Tone Chime & Voice alert sounds on phone!

4. Completed

Order handed over & marked finished.

Web Audio Notification Engine:

When shop staff marks an order as Ready, the student screen plays a 4-tone harmonic chime (E5 - G#5 - B5 - E6), speaks an automated announcement: "Attention! Order token #... is READY at Counter!", and opens the glowing pickup token modal.

Monetization & Profit Architecture

Fintech Revenue Split Architecture (Platform Profit)

Automated packaging fee distribution model for sustainable platform revenue

₹5.00 Pre-Packaging Fee Model

Every pre-order includes a nominal ₹5.00 pre-packaging fee to facilitate pre-packed break readiness.

₹2.50 Platform Profit (Deepak Sharma)

₹2.50 is instantly credited to Platform Founder Deepak Sharma as pure net service commission.

₹2.50 Vendor Packaging Cost Share

₹2.50 is credited to the shop owner to cover food boxes, carry bags, and packing material expenses.

Cash Security & Commission Protection

Auto-Deduction Live Cash Ledger System

Zero commission leakage when students pay cash directly to the shop counter

Cash Payment Challenge

When students pay cash on pickup, the shopkeeper collects 100% of the money. Without a ledger, the platform owner risks losing the ₹2.50 commission share.

Automated Debt Recording

Every cash order automatically records ₹2.50 under the shop's 'Pending Commission Due'. It is automatically deducted from their next online UPI settlement.

Live Multi-Shop Balances

Super Admin dashboard displays live balances for all 23 shops: Total Orders, GMV, Online vs. Cash breakdown, and Net Commission Due per outlet.

1-Click Manual Settlement

Super Admin can click 'Settle Cash Ledger' once physical cash is cleared, generating a permanent timestamped audit log.
