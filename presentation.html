Current Scenario

The Problem: Canteen Rush & Time Loss

15-Minute Break Rush

During short 15-minute lecture breaks, huge rushes at the canteen counter cause massive delays.

Missed Lectures

Students often arrive late to class while waiting in line for simple snacks or practical files.

Cash & Paper Token Confusion

Handling loose cash change and losing paper receipts creates frustration for students and staff.

Project Objective

The Solution: Smart Pre-Ordering Ecosystem

Order from Classroom

Select your meals, snacks, or stationery right from your phone before the class break begins.

Choose Your Pickup Slot

Pick an exact time (e.g. 'In 15 Mins', 'Lunch Break 1:15 PM') that fits your schedule.

Zero-Wait Express Pickup

Walk up to the express counter, show your digital Token ID, collect packed items, and go!

User Workflow

How It Works: In 3 Easy Steps

1

Browse & Add to Cart

Open Campus Mart on your phone, choose your favorite food, chocolates, or stationery, and add them to cart.

2

Pick Time Slot & Order

Select your pickup slot (e.g. 'In 15 Mins' or 'Lunch Break') and get your unique Order Token ID.

3

Express Pickup

Staff packs your order in advance. When the status turns Green (Ready), collect it without waiting in line!

Real-Time Visibility

Live 4-Stage Order Tracking System

1. Placed

Order received by the kitchen and inventory staff.

2. Preparing

Staff is cooking your food or packing your items.

3. Ready for Pickup

Packed on counter. Collect instantly with Token ID!

4. Completed

Order handed over and finished successfully.

How It Works Under the Hood:

The web application performs automatic background polling every 4 seconds. When staff updates the order status in their dashboard, the student's screen turns green in real time without page reload.

Engineering

Technologies Used (Clean & Fast)

Frontend: HTML5 + CSS3 + Vanilla JS

Super fast, responsive mobile-first UI that runs on any smartphone browser without downloading apps.

Backend: Python Flask REST API

Handles user authentication, order token generation, and RESTful API endpoints cleanly.

Database: MongoDB NoSQL

Stores flexible JSON documents for students, multi-item orders, and product catalog.
